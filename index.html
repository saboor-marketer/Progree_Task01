Ask us anything
What does a Mora tonic actually taste like?

Light, dry, and properly sparkling. Think a grown-up botanical soda with real fruit, gentle herbs, and just enough sweetness to feel like a treat. Mora is not syrupy or candy-like — the finish is clean and a little sophisticated.

Is there caffeine in Mora?

Never. Every Mora tonic is caffeine-free, including our adaptogen blend of ashwagandha and L-theanine. It’s made for afternoon resets, slow dinners, long drives, and anywhere else you want a lift without the jitter.

How much sugar is in each can?

About 2g of sugar per can, all of it naturally occurring from real fruit. There is zero added sugar, no artificial sweeteners, and no sugar alcohols. Just a dry, botanical sparkle.

When will my order be delivered?

Your pack is hand-packed and leaves our studio within two business days. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days, and all starter packs ship free with carbon-neutral shipping. We currently ship within the United States.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?

Anytime, with no hard feelings. You can pause, swap flavors, skip a delivery, or cancel completely from your account. Our 30-day feel-good guarantee means you can try Mora with an easy conscience.

Are Mora tonics vegan and gluten-free?

Yes, all three flavors are vegan and gluten-free. We keep our ingredients simple: sparkling water, real fruit, prebiotic fiber, ashwagandha, L-theanine, lemon balm, and natural botanical extracts.
